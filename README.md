# Safety Punks Official Repository: The Breakdown
Safety Punks breaks down workplace regulations and safety standards with the goal of helping uninformed workers understand their workplace safety rights and navigate safety topics without corporate and governmental language. Safety Punks is an educational platform only and does not establish a professional relationship of any kind with its readers.<br>

We don't do "corporate policy." We don't do "synergy." We don't do "covering assets." We do the hard work of protecting the people that make a business run, the ones that actually do the work. This repo discusses safety and industrial hygiene that makes sense for workers and those who want to learn to improve their workplace and safety culture. 

### Safety Punks Setlist
**I. Safety in the Pit:** <br>
If someone falls, you pick them up. Just like in the workplace, we don't protect people for the sake of a corporate insurance premium or because a regulation told us to. We protect our own so everyone goes home in one piece every day.

**II. Compliance is not Enough:** <br>
Compliance sets the bare minimum. It's the floor, not the ceiling, of workplace safety. While the strictness of regulations will always be debated, the bottom line remains the same: we need structure that holds companies and agencies genuinely accountable for the safety of their workers.

**III. DIY 'til I Die:** <br>
Safety Punks encourages workers to take ownership of workplace safety. If a hazard exists, don't ignore it, call it out! Understand the hazards and risks of your daily tasks. Don't wait for your employer to define what's safe. You have the power to identify hazards, assess risks, and suggest to your employer how to control them.

**IV. Screaming at a Wall:** <br>
Safety Punks doesn't speak corporate or recite regulations without explanation. We speak plainly, practically, and directly about workplace safety issues. Information is only useful if people actually understand it.

**V. The Hierarchy of Controls:** <br>
Safety Punks respects science. The Hierarchy of Controls is our foundation for safety thinking, a proven framework that prioritizes the most effective solutions first: Elimination, Substitution, Engineering Controls, Administrative Controls, and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE). PPE is always the last line of defense. Why? Because it does not eliminate the hazard, it only protects the worker from it. PPE becomes the last line of defense when all other controls cannot fully eliminate or reduce the hazard.

**VI. Question Everything** <br>
Question everything. If leadership instructs you to perform a task that feels dangerous or uncomfortable, the Safety Punk inside you has the right to question the job and demand a safer way to get the job done.

**VII. Moshing Knowledge:** <br>
If you don't know your workplace safety rights, it's easy to get run over by leadership. Safety Punks is backed by Certified Industrial Hygienist (CIH) and Certified Safety Professional (CSP) expertise to turn knowledge into shields. We are here to educate those who may be exploited by a corporate safety program. <br>

**VIII. Safety Punk Community** <br>
We are fueled by the energy of punk, hardcore, metal, and all genres of music. Music is essential to our well-being. It brings people together and creates a bond between our passions and energy. Whether you are listening to your favorite album at your desk, in the shop, or in the car, or throwing your best spinkick in the pit, one thing is undeniable: the Safety Punk Community looks out for each other, in the pit and in the workplace. <br>

***Disclaimer:*** *Safety Punks does not provide legal advice, professional consultation, or site-specific safety guidance. The information shared is for general educational purposes only and does not constitute professional advice of any kind. Information may not reflect the most current regulatory updates. Always verify with the appropriate regulatory agency or qualified professional. Regulations and standards vary by industry, location, and jurisdiction. Always consult a qualified Certified Safety Professional, Certified Industrial Hygienist, or legal counsel for guidance specific to your workplace.*
